# EduBridge

### Bridging the gap between students and teachers in digital education.

## About EduBridge

EduBridge is a smart tablet concept designed to address one of the major gaps in digital education: **teachers can see what content a student is accessing, but they often cannot understand how the student is responding to it.**

In a traditional classroom, teachers can naturally observe physical and behavioural cues. They can notice when a student looks confused, loses attention, becomes disengaged or needs additional help. In digital learning, many of these cues are lost because the student is learning through a screen, often away from the teacher.

EduBridge aims to bring this missing layer of awareness back into digital education.

The tablet uses a **hybrid intelligence approach**, where AI observes and analyses student responses while the teacher remains responsible for the final decision. The system can analyse cues related to **engagement, attention, confusion and stress**, while also assisting with repetitive monitoring and assessment-related tasks.

The purpose is not to replace the teacher or make decisions about students automatically. Instead, EduBridge acts as an **intelligent assistant for the teacher**, providing information that may otherwise be difficult to notice during digital learning.

## Core Idea

The basic approach is:

**AI detects → AI provides insight → Teacher verifies → Teacher decides**

For example, if the system detects that a student may be disengaged or confused during a lesson, it can generate an alert on the teacher's dashboard. The teacher can then review the available information and decide whether the student needs an explanation, additional support or no intervention.

This keeps **human judgment at the centre** of the system.

## What EduBridge Tries to Solve

Digital education has made learning more flexible and accessible, but it can also create distance between students and educators.

EduBridge focuses on reducing this gap by giving teachers better visibility into the student's learning experience.

Instead of only asking:

> **"What is the student learning?"**

EduBridge also tries to answer:

> **"How is the student responding to what they are learning?"**

This can help create a more **responsive, personalized and human-centred digital learning environment**.

## Human-in-the-Loop Approach

EduBridge follows a teacher-assisted model rather than allowing AI to make decisions independently.

The AI provides:

- Observations
- Predictions
- Confidence levels
- Alerts
- Explainable insights

The teacher provides:

- Context
- Verification
- Final decisions
- Student intervention

This creates a system where **AI handles analysis and repetitive work, while teachers handle understanding and decisions.**

## Vision

EduBridge aims to make digital education more than just a way of delivering content.

**The goal is to make digital learning understand the student better and help the teacher respond at the right time.**
